Privacy Policy for Fillit

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Fillit is a mobile puzzle game developed by Ulan Sherimbekov.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you use the Fillit mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Fillit does not require you to create an account and does not directly request your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

However, Fillit uses third-party advertising and mediation services. These services may automatically collect certain information from your device, such as:

  • IP address;
  • device type and operating system;
  • advertising identifier;
  • approximate location derived from IP address or device information;
  • information about advertisements displayed and interacted with;
  • technical information necessary to serve and measure advertisements;
  • diagnostic and performance information.

The exact information collected may depend on the advertising network serving an advertisement.

2. Advertising Services

Fillit uses Unity LevelPlay to mediate advertisements from third-party advertising networks, including Unity Ads and Google AdMob.

These services may collect and process device and advertising information to:

  • display advertisements;
  • measure advertising performance;
  • prevent fraud and abuse;
  • provide and improve advertising services.

Third-party advertising providers have their own privacy policies governing their processing of information.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through advertising and technical services may be used to:

  • operate and maintain the game;
  • display advertisements;
  • measure advertising performance;
  • prevent fraud and abuse;
  • improve the stability and performance of the application.

We do not sell users' personal information ourselves.

4. Data Sharing

Fillit may share or allow access to relevant device and advertising information through third-party advertising and mediation providers for the purposes described above.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

5. Data Retention

Fillit does not maintain a personal user account database.

Information collected by third-party advertising providers may be retained according to their respective privacy policies and retention practices.

6. Children's Privacy

Fillit does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. Third-party service providers are responsible for the security of information processed through their services according to their own policies.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

Requests concerning information processed by third-party advertising providers may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

9. Third-Party Privacy Policies

For information about how third-party services process data, please refer to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads
  • Unity LevelPlay

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
